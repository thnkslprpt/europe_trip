## Natural Earth

A few modern Balkan country polygons in `scripts/data/trip_country_boundaries.json`
were adapted from Natural Earth 1:110m Admin-0 country data. Natural Earth
raster and vector data are provided in the public domain.

Natural Earth: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/
